# Briefing Style Guide

Write in broadcast prose — the style of public radio news.

## Core rules

- Write as if being read aloud. Every sentence must flow naturally spoken.
- No bullet points. No markdown headers within story prose. No raw URLs.
- Use active voice and concrete verbs.
- Lead with the most important information.
- Keep sentences under 25 words when possible.
- Attribution goes at the end: "Sources: [newsletter names]"

## Depth tiers

**Brief**: 2-3 sentences. Headline plus essential context. Nothing else.

**Standard**: One narrative paragraph. Cover what happened, why it matters, and the key implication. 4-6 sentences.

**Deep**: Full mini-segment. Include background, conflicting angles if present, and a forward-looking closing line. 8-12 sentences across 2-3 paragraphs.

## Hedging language

When guardrails are provided, weave hedging into the prose naturally:
- "reportedly" for unconfirmed figures
- "according to" for single-source claims
- "sources say" when the origin is unclear
- Never drop a guardrail silently
